This is the adopted display-tag vocabulary that `CONVENTIONS.md` §11.1
(addendum, binding 2026-08-25) cites as "the working inventory's
`tag-apply/adopted-concepts.md`". The file was never written at adoption
time (gap recorded in `HANDOFF.md`); it was reconstructed 2026-08-26 from
`tag-gaps-review.md`, per that gap record.

**Derivation rule:** the 162 §2 proposed-concept entries of
`tag-gaps-review.md` (Themes A–M), adopted in full by Jesse's 2026-08-25
ruling ("I agree with all additions. I'm okay with having more tags as long
as it's helpful" — verbatim postscript on the review file), minus the
review's one recorded fold. Where a §2 heading offers an alternative id
("or ..."), the first-named id is the adopted id; alternative-id,
one-concept-or-two, and lexicon-extension-vs-mint questions remain deferred
to concept-pack curation per §11.7.

**Fold note:** `waiting-and-timing-in-love` folded into
`romantic-love-and-intimacy` (162 proposed → 161 adopted).

**Engine status:** these ids are sanctioned display tags and engine
vocabulary-addition candidates only (§11.1). The engine-built marker below
records whether the id also exists as an engine concept
(`ontology/concepts/<id>.yaml`) on the repo's `origin/main` as of
2026-08-26 (engine census 239); it confers nothing — engine admission is
governed solely by the fixtures/gauntlet/PR-merge covenant.
