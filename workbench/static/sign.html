Sign the baselines

When the engine's identity moves, a human reviewer reads what changed and signs two small records saying "I looked, and this is acceptable." This page walks that review: the workbench computes every digest with the gauntlet's own code, and you write every word. Nothing is committed for you — your commit and Jesse's merge stay the final human gates.

1What you're signing

The engine identity this checkout produces, and the identity the committed approvals still point at.

Reading the current identity (this builds the small fixture database once — about half a minute on first load)…

2What changed

Generate the review evidence: the probe review packet (before/after result lists with every movement marked) and the ordering diff. Read it before signing — that reading is the review.

3Who reviewed it

Every field below is yours to write — the workbench never suggests or fills in reviewer words. The independence policy lives in docs/governance/probe-baseline-review.md, unchanged by this page. Who can sign, quoted from it: the reviewer "did not author the change that moved the baseline — not the data, not the code, not the proposal", "is a distinct identity from the repository owner acting alone on his own machine", and "is designated by Jesse, per review". "A reviewer who cannot truthfully write down what they did not author must not sign."

A stable, accountable handle — who is signing.

How to reach the signer later (email or equivalent).

In your own words: what change is under review, and what part of it you did not author. If you cannot truthfully write this, do not sign.

Why the probe-list movement you just read is acceptable.

Why the full-page ordering movement is acceptable.

This becomes the committed record under docs/reviews/ — your notes, exactly as typed. Quote repository-relative paths only; absolute local paths are refused (the docs-governance guard would fail them later anyway).

4Make it official

First preview the exact three files a write would produce — the review record and the two approval files — then confirm. The approvals are validated with the gauntlet's own validators before anything touches disk.

5Prove it

Run the full gauntlet against what was just written. Expected here: ADMIT WITH WARNINGS with G2 (determinism) and G8 (noise probes) passing.

6Commit it yourself

The workbench never commits. These are the exact files that changed — branch, commit, push, and open a PR. Your merge is the final human gate.
